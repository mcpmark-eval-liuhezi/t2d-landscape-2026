## 5. Reference Case: The SUNSHINE Study (NCT01608724)

The SUNSHINE study — "A Multicenter, Single Arm, Cohort Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Saxagliptin 5mg, Once Daily for 24 Weeks, in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus" — is a **Phase 4**, interventional study sponsored by **AstraZeneca** (INDUSTRY). Its design is a **multicenter, single-arm, non-randomized, open-label (unmasked) cohort study** with a treatment primary purpose: patients received oral saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks. The study enrolled 2,165 participants (actual), and its primary outcome was the absolute change from baseline in HbA1c, assessed at weeks 6, 12, and 24. Participants were either treatment-naïve or inadequately controlled on metformin alone, and the study ran from November 2012 to May 2014, with a completed overall status.
