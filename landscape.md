## 5. Reference case: AstraZeneca SUNSHINE study of saxagliptin (NCT01608724)

- **Design:** Interventional, **Phase 4**
- **Study type:** Interventional, single-arm cohort study; treatment model – single group assignment; **no randomization; no masking**
- **Primary purpose:** Treatment
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (lead sponsor, industry class); responsible party is the sponsor; no data monitoring committee reported
- **Enrollment:** 2,165 (actual)
- **Intervention:** Drug: Saxagliptin (oral, 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks; brand name Onglyza); open-label "Saxagliptin" arm
- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus who were treatment-naïve or inadequately controlled on metformin alone, HbA1c >7.5% and ≤11.0%
- **Primary outcome:** Absolute change from baseline in HbA1c at weeks 6, 12 and 24
- **Key secondary outcomes:** Proportion of patients achieving HbA1c <7%; change from baseline in fasting plasma glucose; change from baseline in 2-hour postprandial plasma glucose
- **Start/primary completion:** Started November 2012; primary completion May 2014 (actual); status COMPLETED
- **Registries:** 92 study centres in China; 3 published outcome figures — at week 6 −1.19%, week 12 −1.59%, week 24 −1.61%, with 44.1% of patients reaching HbA1c <7% at week 24 on per-protocol analysis set (n = 1,210)
- **Collection areas in this snapshot:** 2016-02-02 version holder on the registry record
