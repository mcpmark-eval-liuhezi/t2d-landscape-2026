# Competitive Landscape — Active Type 2 Diabetes Pipeline

This report snapshots the active type 2 diabetes (T2D) pipeline in the ClinicalTrials.gov registry as of today's pull. The active-study set was defined as studies whose overall status is **recruiting**, **not yet recruiting**, or **active but not recruiting**. All counts below are quoted exactly as the registry returned them on the day of this pull (no rounding, no estimates) and are as of this pull.

## Reference case — SUNSHINE (AstraZeneca, saxagliptin), NCT01608724

The PI's tracked reference case is the AstraZeneca-sponsored Phase 4 study of saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks. Per its complete registry record: the industry-sponsor AstraZeneca ran the study under the single-arm, non-randomized, open-label (single-group), treatment-purpose design, with saxagliptin (DPP-4 inhibitor, brand Onglyza) as the sole intervention in patients who were treatment-naive or inadequately controlled on metformin alone. The primary endpoint was the absolute change from baseline in HbA1c (weeks 6, 12, and 24), with a secondary endpoint of the proportion of patients achieving HbA1c <7%, plus change in fasting and 2-hour postprandial plasma glucose. The study enrolled 2,165 patients at 92 sites in China, a study performed 2012–2014 (first posted May 2016 in the registry). SUNSHINE is a post-approval, single-arm real-world-style program — the record frames a DPP-4 inhibitor class versus the emergence of GLP-1 and SGLT2-era options — and is the natural anchor for the class-positioning paragraph in our narrative.
